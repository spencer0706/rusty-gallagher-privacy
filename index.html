Privacy Policy

The Adventures of Rusty Gallagher & Rusty's Roundup

The short version

The Adventures of Rusty Gallagher and Rusty's Roundup don't collect anything about you. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and nothing is ever sent to us.

Your save game

Rusty's progress, your settings and your button layout are kept on your device. If you're signed in to iCloud, a copy of the save goes into your own iCloud account (Apple's iCloud key-value storage) so it follows you between your iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple TV. Apple keeps it for you under your Apple ID. We can't see it.

Rusty's Roundup and Apple Health

With your permission, the Apple Watch app reads your workouts, your Activity ring summaries and whether you've told Health you use a wheelchair (so it can say "pushes" instead of "steps"). It only reads them, on your own devices, to work out which badges you've earned. It never writes to Health.

What leaves your devices

Your health data never does. It isn't sent to us or anyone else, and it's never sold or used for advertising. The only thing the watch saves to your iCloud is the names of the badges you've earned and when, so the game can hand Rusty his rewards.

Changing your mind

You can stop the watch app reading Health at any time: on your iPhone, open Settings > Health > Data Access & Devices > Rusty's Roundup. Settings > Reset progress in the game wipes your save. Deleting the apps, and their data under Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Manage Storage, removes everything.

Children

The apps don't collect personal information from anyone, children included.

TestFlight

If you're testing through TestFlight, Apple may share crash reports and any feedback you choose to send. That's covered by Apple's own privacy policy.

Changes and questions

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be in the game, in the watch app and on the web, with a new date. Questions? Open an issue at github.com/spencer0706/rusty-gallagher-privacy.
